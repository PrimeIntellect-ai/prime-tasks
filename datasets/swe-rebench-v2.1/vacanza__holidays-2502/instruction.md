Missing "Thiruvalluvar Day" and "Uzhavar Thinam" in India (Tamil Nadu) regional holidays
<h1>Holidays Missed in Tamil Nadu, India:</h1>

<b>The day following Pongal, Thiruvalluvar Day.</b>

<b>Two days following Pongal, Uzhavar Thinam.</b>

<h1>Problem:</h1>

At the moment, Pongal and Makar Sankranti are considered to be the same holiday.

In 2003, Makar Sankranti was celebrated on January 14, while Pongal was celebrated on January 15 in Tamil Nadu.

Treating them as the same causes incorrect holiday representation.
