Mapping Source should allow Logicals and Resources
When defining a `Mapping`, the `Source` indicates where the `StructureDefinition` where the mapping statements will be applied.  Currently, SUSHI only allows Profiles/Extensions to be referenced as the `Source`, but it should also allow Logicals and Resources since they are also StructureDefinitions.

See: https://chat.fhir.org/#narrow/stream/215610-shorthand/topic/Logical.20Model.20Mapping
