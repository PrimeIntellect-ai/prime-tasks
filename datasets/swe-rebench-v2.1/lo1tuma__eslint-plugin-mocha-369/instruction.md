Consitent rule naming
Currently we do have some rules related to test descriptions but sometimes we are using the term "description" and sometimes title:

* `no-empty-description`
* `no-identical-title`
* `valid-suite-description`
* `valid-test-description`

### Additional contract requirements

- Use “title” consistently in the related rule identifiers: `no-empty-title`, `no-identical-title`, `valid-suite-title`, and `valid-test-title`.
