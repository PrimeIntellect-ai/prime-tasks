Switzerland - Peter und Paul ist Observance
### Affected country

Switzerland

### Affected public holiday

St. Peter and Paul

### Source of the information

https://www.timeanddate.de/feiertage/schweiz/peter-und-paul

### More information

Peter and Paul will be a public holiday until 2020 and since 2021 observance

### Additional contract requirements

- Classify St. Peter and Paul in Switzerland as a public holiday through 2020 and as an observance from 2021 onward; use `HolidayType.OBSERVANCE` for the observance type.
