### Additional contract requirements

The department service methods `getAssignedDepartmentsOfMember(Person)`,
`getManagedDepartmentsOfDepartmentHead(Person)` and
`getManagedDepartmentsOfSecondStageAuthority(Person)` must return their
departments ordered alphabetically by department name.
