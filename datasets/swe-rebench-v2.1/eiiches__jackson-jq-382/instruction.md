### Additional contract requirements

When a property referenced on the left-hand side of a complex assignment is absent from the context JSON, it must be treated as `null` instead of causing a `NullPointerException`. Concretely, evaluating `.output += [.input[0]+1]` against the input `{"input":[1]}` must produce `{"input":[1],"output":[2]}`: the absent `output` is treated as `null`, and `null + [2]` yields `[2]`.
