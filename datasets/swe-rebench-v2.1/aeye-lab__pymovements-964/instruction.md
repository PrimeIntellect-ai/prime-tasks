Add the Frank et al. (2013) data

The data is available with the paper:

Frank, S.L., Fernandez Monsalve, I., Thompson, R.L. et al. Reading time data for evaluating broad-coverage models of English sentence processing. Behav Res 45, 1182–1190 (2013). https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-012-0313-y

### Additional contract requirements

The paper's data must be available through the dataset facilities as precomputed event data and reading-time measures; the paper-data integration does not include raw gaze recordings. Their resources must be obtainable through configured download locations and recognizable for loading by the dataset facilities. The integration must be publicly available as `pm.datasets.UCL` and registered under the dataset name `UCL`.
