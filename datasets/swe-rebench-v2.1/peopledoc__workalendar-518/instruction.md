Croatia new National Holiday and Independence day moved
Hello,

there were some changes in 2019. for national holidays in Croatia. Holidays listed [here](https://www.officeholidays.com/countries/croatia/2020) are correct. This means that the following changes need to be made:
1. `Statehood day` needs to be moved from Jun 25 to May 30,
2. A new holiday named `Remembrance Day` must be added on Nov 18.

### Additional contract requirements

- Keep October 8 as a Croatian holiday through 2019; from 2020 onward, it is no longer a holiday.
- Statehood Day remains on June 25 through 2019. From 2020 onward, it is observed on May 30 instead.
- Remembrance Day on November 18 is a Croatian holiday starting in 2020, and is not a holiday before then.
