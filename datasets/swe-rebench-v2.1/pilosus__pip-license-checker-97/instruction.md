zlib/libpng is detected, but zlib is not
The checker detects [zlib/libpng](https://spdx.org/licenses/zlib-acknowledgement.html) (a.k.a. zlib with acknowledgement).
But it doesn't detects the [zlib](https://spdx.org/licenses/Zlib.html)

The latter is a more permissive version of `zlib/libpng`, but these two still considered independent licenses

### Additional contract requirements

- Classify `zlib/whatever-new` as `license/type-other` (`Other`).
