Support PSF license(s)
We need to support the [PSF-2.0](https://github.com/python/cpython/blob/main/LICENSE) at least.

Older licenses like `Python-2.0`, `Python-2.0.1`, `CNRI-Python`, `CNRI-Python-GPL-Compatible` are probably not really needed as they are from the [pre-2001](https://github.com/python/cpython/blob/main/LICENSE) era.
