Display decimals when over 1 million
:clipboard: **Description**

Once downloads hit 1 million, the resolution of downloads is simply an integer, e.g. 1M. Pretty big milestone for some projects, but getting to display 2M might take just as long if not longer. What about showing a single decimal digit, e.g. 1.2M which still fits within limits. Perhaps this drops off after 10M. Same would go for billions, etc.
