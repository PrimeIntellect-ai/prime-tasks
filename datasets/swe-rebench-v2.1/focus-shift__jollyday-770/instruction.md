Austria: Martin of Tours Day, Josef of Nazaret Day, Day of the plebiscite, Leopold III. day, Florian of Lorch's Day, Rupert of Salzburg's day are no public holiday
### Affected country

Austria

### Affected public holiday

Martin of Tours Day, Josef of Nazaret Day, Day of the plebiscite, Leopold III. day, Florian of Lorch's Day, Rupert of Salzburg's day

### Source of the information

https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feiertage_in_%C3%96sterreich

### More information

Martin of Tours Day, Josef of Nazaret Day, Day of the plebiscite, Leopold III. Day, Florian of Lorch's day, Rupert of Salzburg's day are no public holiday in Austria. They are of the type observance.

### Additional contract requirements

- For Florian of Lorch's Day in Oberösterreich, tests require it to be an observance on May 4 from 2004 through 2500 inclusive, and not valid from 1900 through 2003 inclusive.
