### Additional contract requirements

- Easter Monday is identified by the holiday property key `christian.EASTER_MONDAY`.
- Easter Monday must be observed in Switzerland's Vaud subdivision.
- Easter Monday must not be observed in Switzerland's Valais subdivision.
