Disallowing `isset` calls
Currently, [certain language constructs can be disallowed](https://github.com/spaze/phpstan-disallowed-calls/blob/27849e500f301a83e720c94c83de738a11a65040/docs/custom-rules.md#language-constructs-and-constructors), though `isset` is not among them.

I'm happy to implement support for `isset` if it aligns with the library's goals, just let me know.
