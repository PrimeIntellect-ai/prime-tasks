Russia public holidays are shifted if happening on SAT/SUN
as described in https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_holidays_in_Russia and confirmed by https://www.timeanddate.com/calendar/custom.html?year=2020&country=20&cols=3&hol=1&df=1, for example, holidays should be shifted to the next MON when required.

### Additional contract requirements

- For 2020, the shifted holidays must have these exact labels: February 24, "Defendence of the Fatherland shift"; March 9, "International Women's Day shift"; May 11, "Victory Day shift".
- - For 2020, the original holiday entries must also remain present on February 23, March 8, and May 9.
