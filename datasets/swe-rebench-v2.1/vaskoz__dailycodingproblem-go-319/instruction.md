Day 153
Good morning! Here's your coding interview problem for today.

Find an efficient algorithm to find the smallest distance (measured in number of words) between any two given words in a string.

For example, given words "hello", and "world" and a text content of "dog cat hello cat dog dog hello cat world", return 1 because there's only one word "cat" in between the two words.

### Additional contract requirements

Provide the callable function `SmallestDistanceBetweenWords(text, one, two string) int` to compute the minimum number of words strictly between occurrences of the two given words. Use an efficient algorithm for texts with many occurrences of both words.
