Route pedestrians over highway=platform
Please allow pedestrians to be routed over `highway=platform`. (This tag should be treated the same as `highway=footway`.) Currently, they are diverted onto the street, see e.g. [here](https://graphhopper.com/maps/?point=46.94326%2C7.44836&point=46.943963%2C7.448967&locale=en-us&vehicle=foot&weighting=fastest&elevation=true&use_miles=false&layer=Omniscale).
