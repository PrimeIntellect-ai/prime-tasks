Sanitize the Pipe (|) Character in Titles
Hi there! I noticed that the pipe character is often included on page titles, but this is difficult to deal with in Obsidian because the pipe character is used in Wikilinks to alias the name. Would it be possible to add the pipe character to the list of sanitized characters?

### Additional contract requirements

- When sanitizing a title, replace the pipe character (`|`) with a hyphen (`-`).
