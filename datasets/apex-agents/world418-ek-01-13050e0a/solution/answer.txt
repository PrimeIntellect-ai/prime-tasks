No, assigning the shipbuilding contract to BlueLNG JV LLC does not require any other member consents under the JV Operating Agreement.

Under Section 2.1(a), the assignment of the shipbuilding contract is LNG Shipping's initial capital contribution to the JV, and both members already agreed to it by signing the Operating Agreement. Accepting that initial capital contribution is not a Reserved Matter under Section 3.3, so it does not require any further member consent. No other provision of the Operating Agreement imposes an additional member-consent requirement on this assignment, and the Builder's consent required by the Assignment Agreement is a third-party consent, not a member consent.
