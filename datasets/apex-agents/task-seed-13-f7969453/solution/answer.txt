Yes. Northstar can reasonably treat the one or two hypothetical log exports as consistent with Section 5.6 of its PRIV-02 V.2 Article 49 derogation policy.

The exports were urgent support actions to prevent serious operational impacts, because engineering could not diagnose the EU production failure without them and the platform was down for every EU customer. The exports were occasional and non-repetitive because there were only one or two bundled exports, nothing was configured to stream on an ongoing basis, and no further exports followed. No other Chapter V mechanism was reasonably available in time because no adequacy decision covers the recipient and the standard contractual clauses could not have been executed inside the outage window. The derogation was documented and approved as soon as feasible because the engineer wrote up the basis the same evening and Privacy signed off seven hours after the exports, once the incident bridge had stood down.
