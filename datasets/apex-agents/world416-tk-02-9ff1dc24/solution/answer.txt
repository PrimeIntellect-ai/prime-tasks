No additional Form 8-K is required solely for the projected 20% gross-margin decline for the remainder of fiscal 2025. Item 2.02 applies to material nonpublic information about results or financial condition for a completed quarterly or annual fiscal period. This forecast concerns future performance.

TAC may nevertheless voluntarily disclose the update by furnishing a Form 8-K under Item 7.01, as it did with its July 1, 2025 guidance update, or by filing under Item 8.01.

Website posting is not automatically sufficient for Regulation FD. If TAC has made a covered selective disclosure, it must provide timely, broad, non-exclusionary public disclosure. A qualifying website disclosure can satisfy that duty without an 8-K. The fact that TAC has posted there before does not, by itself, establish compliance.
