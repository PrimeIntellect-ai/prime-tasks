The year with the highest average stay length for Bronze Tier members is 2022, and the year with the lowest average stay length is 2025. For Bronze Tier members who stayed at a Summit Express property, the average stay lengths segmented by credit card co-branding are as follows:

## Lowest Average Stay Year (2025):
