You are the newly appointed Supply Chain Manager at GlobalSport Distribution Inc., a sporting goods company shipping globally from two distribution centers. The CFO has escalated a capital expenditure request: the VP of Operations wants $1.2M to expand the San Juan DC by 3,000 pallet positions and hire 12 additional warehouse staff. Before the board approves the spend, the CFO wants an independent assessment of whether the existing network is optimized — or whether operational improvements could absorb projected demand growth without capital expansion. You have 48 hours before the board meeting.

Deliverable 1: Network Efficiency & Capital Justification Analysis (Excel workbook, .xlsx)

Build an Excel workbook with four sheets. The "Capacity_Utilization_Model" sheet should model the effective throughput utilization of each DC by calculating actual versus theoretical capacity, determining whether the San Juan DC's reported utilization reflects genuine constraints or operational inefficiency. Estimate the recoverable capacity if waste and safety stock parameters were brought to target levels. The "Demand_Allocation_Analysis" sheet should analyze shipment volume by warehouse-region pair across all three years to identify meaningful growth corridors and assess whether on-time delivery performance is deteriorating where it matters. The "Reorder_Optimization_Scenario" sheet should evaluate whether current safety stock and reorder points are calibrated correctly for each department-warehouse combination, flagging where over-stocking is consuming capacity. The "Investment_Recommendation" sheet should present two scenarios — approve the expansion as-is versus defer via operational improvements — with impact projections and a payback comparison.

Deliverable 2: Revised Warehouse SOP with Integrated Change Justification (Word document, .docx)

Review the current warehouse SOP alongside your quantitative findings. Produce a revised process document that restructures around operational failures uncovered in the data, with each revised section referencing the specific metric or finding justifying the change. Include a phased implementation timeline with dependencies between phases, a capacity recovery projection table linking each process improvement to quantified impact, and a KPI governance framework with metric owners, escalation thresholds, and review cadence tied to rollout milestones.

You will also need to obtain two supplementary files from the external "Just In Time" company supply chain analytics dataset: inventory.csv (monthly warehouse inventory levels and cost per unit) and fulfillment.csv (product-level warehouse order fulfillment days).
