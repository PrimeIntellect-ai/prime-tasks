You are a management analyst engaged by Meridian Advanced Manufacturing Inc., a mid-size manufacturer operating four U.S. plants (Columbus OH, Nashville TN, Phoenix AZ, Portland OR). VP of Operations David Chen has commissioned an operational efficiency review covering Q1/Q2 2024. His memo outlines specific concerns and figures — verify these against actual data before citing them in your deliverables.

The board has raised concerns about margin compression and uneven plant performance and wants to know whether consolidating production across fewer plants might improve margins. Produce three deliverables.

The first deliverable is a diagnostic analysis workbook in Excel (.xlsx) containing five tabs. The "Plant Scorecard" tab should present per-plant OEE, defect rate per 1,000 units, average on-time delivery percentage, unplanned downtime as a share of total hours, and cost per unit, with plants ranked by composite operational health. "Trend Diagnostics" should flag plant–product line combinations deteriorating month-over-month on at least two of defect rate, OTD%, and target attainment as watch items, and compute 6-month trend slopes for each plant's aggregate defect rate and OTD%. The "Industry Benchmark" tab should compare each plant's capacity utilization against the national manufacturing average derived from the Federal Reserve's publicly available MCUMFN series for January through June 2024, obtained separately, and position each plant within the 25th/50th/75th percentile distributions of DefectRate and DowntimePercentage from the included industry defect benchmarking dataset. "Complaint-Production Crossref" should join complaint records to production data by plant and month, computing complaint rates per 1,000 units and assessing whether downtime correlates with complaint severity. The "Equipment Risk Matrix" should cross-reference equipment age, condition rating, and overhaul history against plant-level downtime and defects, flagging items where age exceeds 8 years and condition is rated 5 or below as replacement priorities with estimated capital needs.

The second deliverable is a cost of poor quality model saved as "Meridian_COPQ_Analysis.csv," quantifying quality failure costs per plant. Compute scrap cost as scrap units multiplied by average raw material cost per unit, rework cost as reworked units multiplied by 40% of average labor cost per unit, return cost as returns multiplied by average material cost per unit, complaint cost weighted by severity (Critical $5,000, High $2,500, Medium $1,000, Low $250), and downtime cost as unplanned hours multiplied by average hourly labor cost derived from total labor divided by total actual hours. Include total COPQ, COPQ as a percentage of total production cost, and a summary row aggregating all plants.

The third deliverable is a 2–3 page strategic investment memo in Word (.docx) addressed to David Chen. Ground recommendations in computed figures from the first two deliverables, prioritize interventions with estimated impact, and directly address the board's consolidation question using evidence from the cost structure, capacity analysis, equipment condition, and workforce data.
