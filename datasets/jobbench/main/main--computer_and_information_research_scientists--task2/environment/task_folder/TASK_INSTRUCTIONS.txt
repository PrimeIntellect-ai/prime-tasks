You are the Director of Research Computing at a mid-sized R1 university. The provost has asked you to evaluate a proposed GPU-accelerated HPC cluster for AI research, but the CFO argues a cloud-only strategy would be more cost-effective. Settle this debate by auditing the planning documents for internal consistency, then building a rigorous comparative analysis across on-premises, cloud-only, and hybrid deployment strategies.

Deliver a data integrity audit as data_audit.md. Before any strategic analysis, audit all provided files for cross-file and intra-file consistency — verify that vendor-quoted specifications, lead times, and pricing are mutually consistent and align with the project schedule's dependency chains and dates. Cross-reference the researcher survey database against proposed per-node capacities to identify any workloads that exceed what the cluster can serve. Present every discrepancy found with severity classification and resolution recommendations.

Deliver a comparative strategy analysis as strategy_analysis.md. Query the survey database to characterize workload profiles by type, GPU-hour volumes, wait-time distributions, and peak-to-average demand. Compute a 3-year cloud-only cost using $2.50/GPU-hr for H100-equivalent and $1.20/GPU-hr for A100-equivalent instances, tiering researchers by GPU memory requirements (>48 GB maps to H100, ≤48 GB to A100). For the hybrid option, assign sustained workloads (>500 GPU-hrs/month per researcher) on-prem and route bursty workloads to cloud, then right-size the on-prem footprint and compute blended costs. Score all three options on a weighted matrix: Cost 30%, Performance 20%, Scalability 15%, Data Sovereignty 10%, Sustainability 15%, Satisfaction Risk 10%. Ground the sustainability dimension in facility-class PUE benchmarks from the publicly available LBNL 2024 United States Data Center Energy Usage Report (Shehabi et al., Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, LBNL-2001637, December 2024). Benchmark the university's $8.2M CS R&D expenditure against NSF HERD peers to contextualize the proposed investment scale.

Deliver an adjusted operational budget as adjusted_budget.xlsx with four sheets — capital costs presented on an upfront procurement cash basis and reflecting a 5% procurement discount and 10% contingency, annual operating costs (power at $0.09/kWh scaled by facility PUE of 1.5, two FTE system administrators at $95K each, $45K in software licenses, vendor support contracts, plus any cloud spend for hybrid or cloud strategies), funding sources, and a summary dashboard showing 3-year TCO per strategy, cost-per-GPU-hour, and break-even utilization rate. Carry the internally consistent electrical baseline established in data_audit.md into the operating budget; a vendor roll-up identified as arithmetically inconsistent may be retained only as a clearly labeled sensitivity case, not as the primary budget input. Treat the quoted $185,000 three-year premium support contract as a prepaid procurement cash outlay already included once in the $2,713,000 vendor subtotal. Show the contract in the annual operating-cost schedule as prepaid coverage with $0 additional cash outlay in Years 1-3, and do not add or annualize it again in the 3-year TCO.

Deliver a corrected project schedule as revised_schedule.csv that resolves every timing and dependency conflict identified in your audit, adds critical_path, risk_level, and audit_note columns, and completes within nine months from project start.
