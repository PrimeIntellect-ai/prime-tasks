Prepare an engineer's estimate for the County Road 42 bridge deck replacement over Clear Creek (Structure F-17-DK, Jefferson County, CDOT Region 1). The project memo defines the authorized scope. Preliminary quantities in Bridge_CR42_Dimensions_and_Quantities.csv were compiled by a junior technician without QA review — independently verify every computed quantity against the bridge dimensions and document all discrepancies. Where the CSV conflicts with your calculations, use corrected values.

The estimate requires three deliverables. First, an Excel workbook (.xlsx) with four tabs: a data verification reconciliation (CSV value vs. your calculated value, percent difference, resolution note — flag deviations exceeding 2%), a quantity takeoff with formulas applying the rounding conventions in the Alaska DOT Bridge Quantity Cost Guide, an engineer's estimate using CDOT's 2024 Item Code Book with unit prices sourced from the externally obtained CDOT 2024 Cost Data Book and cross-referenced against the externally obtained Michigan MDOT 2024 LAP Bridge Cost Estimating Worksheet or Alaska DOT Table 8-2 where CDOT data is unavailable, and a cost summary with mobilization at 10%, contingency at 20%, and construction engineering at 15% of the total including mobilization.

Second, a value engineering comparison (.csv) of full deck replacement versus HPC overlay using the externally sourced Michigan LAP "Deep Overlay" cost adjusted for Region 1 conditions, with a 30-year lifecycle recommendation.

Third, a summary memo (.docx, one page maximum) covering total cost, principal cost drivers, data quality findings, and risks requiring further design input.
