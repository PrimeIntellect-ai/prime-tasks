You are the Supply Chain Manager at the Montgomery County Beverage Distribution Center in Maryland. The facility distributes wine, beer, liquor, and non-alcoholic beverages to retail outlets across the county, handling approximately 2,800 SKUs across five major suppliers. Current performance stands at 6.2x inventory turns against an 8–12x industry benchmark, 4.2% dead stock, and a 91.3% customer fill rate.

The VP of Supply Chain has called an urgent quarterly business review. Contracts with Republic National and Winebow expire within 60 days. Operations reports persistent stockouts on high-velocity items while warehouse space is consumed by slow movers. You must prepare a unified, data-driven recommendation package — the VP expects an integrated analysis connecting inventory performance to supplier performance, feeding into contract and process decisions.

Prepare an Excel workbook (.xlsx) with three interlinked sheets. The first sheet should cross-tabulate ABC-classified SKUs by supplier, showing SKU counts, revenue share, average inventory turns per cell, and dead stock concentration by supplier. The second sheet should analyze demand volatility for your top-tier items and flag reorder point misalignments where current static levels deviate from supplier-lead-time-appropriate coverage by more than 30%, with proposed adjustments. The third sheet should produce a weighted supplier scorecard — on-time delivery rate (30%), order accuracy (25%), average lead time versus contract terms (20%), quality issue rate (15%), and year-over-year spend trend (10%) — with a renewal recommendation for the two expiring contracts.

Produce a contract negotiation brief (.docx) for Republic National and Winebow. For each supplier, synthesize all available evidence into a recommended negotiation position backed by specific numeric targets drawn from your analysis. If a supplier's performance does not justify renewal, recommend transition planning and estimate the SKU migration scope.

Review the current replenishment SOP and produce a redline revision (.docx) that retains the original section structure, presenting changes as tracked revisions or clearly marked redline annotations so reviewers can see exactly what changed. The revision should implement tiered replenishment frequencies driven by your classification results, replace static reorder points with demand-and-lead-time-driven calculations referencing actual supplier lead times, and address documented process inefficiencies by their reference numbers. Include performance targets (inventory turns ≥ 9x, fill rate ≥ 97%, dead stock ≤ 1.5%) with a monitoring cadence and escalation triggers.

Montgomery County warehouse-level beverage sales transaction data is provided locally as warehouse_sales_2019_2020.csv for use alongside the inventory, supplier performance, and process.
