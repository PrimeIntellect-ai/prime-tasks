You are a Training and Development Specialist at Precision Manufacturing Inc., a mid-sized manufacturing company with 350 employees. Following a recent OSHA compliance audit and insurance carrier review, leadership has escalated concerns: safety incidents increased 15% and quality defects rose 20% last quarter. The insurance carrier has requested documentation demonstrating a comprehensive training improvement plan by end of Q1 2025.

Complete the OSHA Safety and Health Program Implementation Checklist as a gap analysis ("OSHA_Gap_Analysis_Completed.pdf"), cross-referencing historical training effectiveness data to justify your assessment of each section's compliance status.

Create a Q2 2025 integrated training schedule ("Q2_2025_Training_Schedule.xlsx") that covers all 180 production floor employees in Safety Orientation, addresses instructor certification continuity risks, and includes reinforcement sessions for programs showing declining behavioral outcomes. Include a constraint resolution column documenting scheduling decisions.

Develop a Kirkpatrick-based evaluation plan ("Q2_Evaluation_Strategy.docx") for the Safety Orientation program that adapts standard evaluation instruments for the manufacturing floor context. The plan should include a delayed survey targeting Level 3 behavior metrics, a supervisor observation rubric, and diagnostic elements for the lowest-performing training program.

Prepare a training ROI analysis workbook ("Training_ROI_Analysis.xlsx") that calculates cost-effectiveness across programs using available metrics, projects Q2 2025 budget requirements for 200 employees across safety and quality training, and identifies break-even points based on incident reduction data.

Create an instructor capacity risk matrix ("Instructor_Risk_Matrix.xlsx") mapping specializations against critical programs, identifying single points of failure, flagging certifications expiring within 180 days, and proposing cross-training recommendations to address coverage gaps.

Use Smartsheet's Training Attendance template (IC-Training-Attendance-8872) as reference formatting for attendance tracking. All deliverables must demonstrate integration across provided data sources with clear traceability between metrics and recommendations.
