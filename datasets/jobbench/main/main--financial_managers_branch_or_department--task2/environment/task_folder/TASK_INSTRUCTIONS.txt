You are the Branch Financial Manager of Meridian Community Bank's Westfield Branch ($820M total assets, ~$50M branch loan portfolio). It is January 2025; Q4 2024 reporting has closed.

The Board has imposed two constraints effective immediately: Westfield's Q1 2025 new lending capacity is capped at $3.0 million, and the single-sector concentration ceiling is lowered to 20% of total outstanding. Existing exposures exceeding 20% are grandfathered until their next annual review.

The CCO has called an emergency Portfolio Review Committee meeting. Prepare the following deliverables.

PART 1 — Enhanced Quarterly Risk Report (Excel .xlsx): Using the branch loan portfolio data and the FDIC's Q4 2024 Quarterly Banking Profile loan performance statistics, produce a portfolio summary dashboard with outstanding balances, concentration percentages, weighted average rates by type, and total portfolio yield. Include an asset quality section that cross-tabulates delinquency by payment status and risk rating, calculates the branch NPA ratio, benchmarks it against the FDIC industry noncurrent rate, and provides variance commentary. Analyze sector concentrations against the 20% ceiling, flag breaches and grandfathering status, and identify the top five largest individual exposures with single-borrower concentration analysis. Build a scored watch list for all adversely rated loans applying the CDFI risk rating worksheet, incorporating both objective factor scores and subjective adjustments mapped to the CDFI scale, with action items grounded in the OCC Comptroller's Handbook on rating credit risk. Model a +200bp parallel rate shock across the watch list, recalculating DSCR under stressed conditions and flagging loans that breach the coverage threshold.

PART 2 — Credit Decision Memoranda with Capital Constraint (PDF): Evaluate the six pending applications against the $3.0M cap. Each memorandum must include a recommendation, CDFI risk score with full scoring detail, credit analysis, regulatory alignment citing the OCC handbook, portfolio impact on concentrations and remaining capacity, and structuring terms for any conditional approvals. Aggregate approvals must respect the lending cap without creating new sector breaches.

PART 3 — Executive Summary (1–2 pages within the Part 2 document): Address the CCO and Board on portfolio health benchmarked against FDIC data with specific variance figures, the top three risks with regulatory-referenced remediation steps, and the capital allocation rationale showing committed versus remaining capacity and projected post-lending impact on asset quality and concentration profile.

The following external resources must be consulted separately:
• FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile, Q4 2024 — loan performance statistics (available from fdic.gov under Quarterly Banking Profile)
• OCC Comptroller's Handbook: "Rating Credit Risk" booklet (available from occ.treas.gov under Publications and Resources)
