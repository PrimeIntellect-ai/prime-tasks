You are the Purchasing Agent for Maplewood County, Texas. Requisition REQ-2025-IT-0892 covers an 85-workstation technology refresh across three floors. Four vendors responded to RFQ #FY2025-IT-0347. After bids were received, the IT Director submitted an addendum (it_director_addendum_email.txt) that revises monitor quantities, adds a VGA requirement for conference room docking stations, compresses the deployment timeline, and introduces supplemental carryover funds with a fiscal expiration constraint. Reconcile all sources — including the external source of applicable municipal purchasing policy (the City of Addison, Texas purchasing manual, 2024 edition) — and produce the deliverables described below.

Prepare a Bid Analysis Workbook (Excel) containing three sheets. The Bid Tabulation sheet presents side-by-side vendor pricing recalculated against the addendum's revised monitor configuration; where a vendor did not quote the required size, estimate substitution cost from their pricing patterns and flag specification gaps against both original and revised requirements. The Weighted Scoring Matrix scores each vendor across six criteria — Price (25%), Technical Compliance (20%), Delivery/Lead Time (20%), Past Performance (15%), Vendor Risk Profile (10%), and HUB Preference (10%) — using the provided evaluation template as the structural basis, vendor performance history for scoring, and compliance flag records for risk inputs. The Cost Summary computes total cost of ownership per vendor incorporating revised quantities, FOB-dependent freight (estimate 3.5% for origin-shipped goods), warranty normalization to a uniform coverage period, the addendum's conference room adapter requirement, and quoted volume discounts; flag any vendor exceeding the revised budget ceiling.

Prepare a Purchase Order (Excel) numbered PO-2025-0412 for the recommended vendor. The PO must reflect revised quantities and spec adjustments, the compressed delivery deadline, and dual budget code allocation (110-5400-53100 for the base appropriation; 110-5400-53100-R for the carryover supplement). Structure the carryover-funded portion as a separable section given its fiscal expiration.

Prepare a Risk and Compliance Assessment (Excel) with three sheets. Delivery Risk compares each vendor's quoted lead times against the available delivery window, classifies risk severity, and cross-references historical on-time performance. Compliance Review evaluates HUB certification status, active compliance flags, contract vehicle validity, and eligibility under the purchasing manual's non-performing vendor provisions. Budget Sensitivity models two scenarios — full award with carryover secured versus fallback award without carryover — and identifies specific descope candidates with dollar impacts.

Prepare an Award Recommendation Memo (Word) of one to two pages addressed to the Purchasing Manager. The memo should present the solicitation scope incorporating addendum impacts, summarize evaluation results, provide a best-value justification grounded in the weighted scoring analysis, address any vendor eligibility concerns arising from unresolved compliance issues, assess the carryover fund timing risk, state conditions of award, and note the governing body approval dependency.
