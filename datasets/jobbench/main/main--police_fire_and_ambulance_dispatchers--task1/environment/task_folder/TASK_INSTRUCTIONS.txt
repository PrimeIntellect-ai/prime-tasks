EMERGENCY DISPATCH OPERATIONS - MULTI-INCIDENT COORDINATION SCENARIO

It is January 15, 2025, 09:00 AM. You are the shift supervisor at Washington D.C. Fire and Emergency Medical Services (FEMS) dispatch center during a surge of simultaneous incidents. Review incoming_calls_queue.csv for 15 pending calls requiring immediate triage; the automated priority assignments may contain errors based on incomplete caller information and require professional re-evaluation.

Cross-reference calls against unit_status.csv for availability, dc_fire_stations.geojson for proximity calculations, and dispatch_cad.db for existing active incidents. Pay particular attention to IC-2025-0115-013 (WATER_RESCUE at Potomac River), IC-2025-0115-004 (GAS_LEAK with symptomatic occupants), and the cluster of cardiac/medical P1 calls in the NW sector.

Create dispatch_decisions.xlsx with Sheet 1 containing your prioritized dispatch queue with adjusted priorities, assigned units, and justification for each decision. Sheet 2 should present a unit-to-incident matching matrix distinguishing capability-based eligibility from proximity-based recommendations.

Using dispatch_cad.db, detect duplicate or related incidents at overlapping addresses, identify units approaching double-booking conflicts or end-of-shift constraints, insert all 15 new calls with your dispatch assignments, and generate conflict_report.csv documenting any anomalies detected during synchronization.

Prepare shift_handover.xlsx for the incoming supervisor. Sheet 1 (Active Incident Map Data) should export GIS-ready coordinates for all active incidents with assigned units and status. Sheet 2 (Resource Depletion Analysis) must calculate unit availability percentages by battalion and flag any specialty capabilities with zero available coverage. Sheet 3 (Call Pattern Analysis) requires historical incident data to identify peak categories, top incident types, and geographic clustering patterns by township. For this pattern analysis, use the external Montgomery County, PA 911 emergency call records from the Kaggle "Emergency - 911 Calls" dataset (montcoalert). Sheet 4 (Operational Notes) should document any deviations from standard closest-unit dispatch protocol with rationale.
