You are a Mechanical Engineering Technician at a petrochemical equipment supplier. Your company's Model CP-150 centrifugal pump (Tag P-2401A) failed its initial factory acceptance test on January 15, 2025, due to excessive vibration. The manufacturer modified the impeller and retested on January 17, 2025. You must evaluate whether the rework was effective, determine final acceptance, and predict field performance using data from the identical sister pump P-2401B already in service. Vibration test data, pump specifications, and sister pump field records are provided; you will also need to consult the Europump pump vibration standards guidelines separately as an external source to complete your analysis.

Reconcile the applicable vibration acceptance limits across the contractual specification and relevant international standards, clarifying which limits govern and why. Perform a systematic point-by-point comparison of the original test against the retest at all shared measurement locations and flow conditions, quantifying improvements and identifying any remaining concerns. Using the sister pump's field baseline, trend history, and maintenance records, assess whether P-2401A's factory signatures are consistent with good field condition and identify any patterns that warrant attention during commissioning.

Make a formal accept, conditional-accept, or reject recommendation for the retest data. Produce a Non-Conformance Report as a structured CSV with these fields: NCR number, date, equipment tag, description, applicable standard clause, measured value, acceptance limit, proposed disposition (use-as-is/rework/reject), and justification.

Deliver an integrated assessment report including an executive summary with your acceptance recommendation, a comparison table with delta values and zone classifications, field performance prediction, recommended commissioning setpoints, pre-installation requirements, and two plots: (a) an overlay of original versus retest vibration across flow conditions for pump-end bearings, and (b) sister pump trend data with projected trajectory.
