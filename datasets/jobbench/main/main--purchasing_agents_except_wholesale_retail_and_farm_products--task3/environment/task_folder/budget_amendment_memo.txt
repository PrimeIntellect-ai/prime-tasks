GREENFIELD TECHNOLOGIES INC.
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

TO:      Procurement Department
FROM:    Michelle Park, Director of Finance
DATE:    January 20, 2025
RE:      Budget Amendment — PR-2025-0042 (Corporate Office Refresh Phase 2)

This memo serves as formal notification of a budget adjustment for the above-referenced purchase requisition.

Due to Q4 2024 capital expenditure overruns in the IT infrastructure upgrade program, the Finance Committee has approved a revised capital budget allocation for FY2025 furniture acquisitions. Effective immediately, the following changes apply:

1. REVISED BUDGET CAP: The approved budget for PR-2025-0042 is reduced from $350,000 to $310,000 (pre-tax). This represents an 11.4% reduction from the original authorization.

2. CONTINGENCY: The 10% contingency reserve referenced in the original PR is eliminated under the revised allocation. All costs must fall within the $310,000 hard cap.

3. SCOPE PRESERVATION: The Facilities team has confirmed that all 150 workstations (desks + chairs) must be procured in full. However, the following categories are eligible for quantity reduction or deferral:
   - Filing cabinets: May be reduced from 75 to 50 units
   - Reception/lounge seating: May be reduced from 30 to 20 units
   - Conference tables and chairs: quantities must remain as specified

4. PHASED PAYMENT PREFERENCE: Finance strongly prefers Net 30 or Net 45 payment terms. Any vendor requiring deposits or accelerated payment schedules must be flagged for CFO review and may require a separate approval workflow that could delay the project by 2-3 weeks.

5. INSTALLATION BUDGET: Installation costs are now included within the $310,000 cap — they are NOT a separate budget line. Any vendor quote that excludes installation must have estimated installation costs factored into the total cost comparison.

Please adjust your vendor evaluation and purchase recommendation accordingly. The original PR approval signatures remain valid; a revised PR form is not required for budget reductions. However, if the recommended vendor's total cost (including installation) cannot be brought within the $310,000 cap, escalation to the VP of Operations is required before issuing a PO.

Questions may be directed to my office.

Michelle Park
Director of Finance
