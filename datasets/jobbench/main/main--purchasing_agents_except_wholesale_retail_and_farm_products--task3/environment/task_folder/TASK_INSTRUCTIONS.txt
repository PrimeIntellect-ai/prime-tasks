You are a Purchasing Agent at Greenfield Technologies Inc. in Austin, TX. The Facilities Manager submitted PR-2025-0042 for a 150-employee office furniture refresh across three floors. Three vendors responded with quotations. After initial approval at $350,000, Finance issued a budget amendment (budget_amendment_memo.txt) reducing the cap to $310,000 (pre-tax, installation inclusive, no contingency). The company maintains a procurement compliance checklist ("compliance_requirements.csv") and a historical vendor performance database ("vendor_history.db") for use in your analysis.

Produce four deliverables:

A discrepancy and compliance audit as a CSV covering all three quotations checked against the purchase requisition and applicable compliance requirements. Flag quantity mismatches, arithmetic errors, specification gaps against mandatory requirements (reference each Requirement_ID), and items requiring verification. Include severity classification and recommended actions.

An adjusted bid tabulation in Excel using the provided template. Consolidate all three quotations side by side and present three scenarios: original totals, adjusted totals reflecting the budget amendment's permitted quantity reductions, and adjusted totals inclusive of installation and freight. Derive installation rates from each vendor's quotation notes. Add comparative columns showing lowest price per item and savings spread across vendors.

A vendor evaluation scorecard in Excel using the provided template. Score each vendor 1–5 across these weighted criteria: Unit Pricing (20%), Total Cost with Installation (15%) benchmarked against the budget cap, Delivery Lead Time & Phased Capability (15%), Warranty Coverage (10%), Past Performance (20%) drawn from historical order data including delivery reliability and any open warranty claims, Compliance Pass Rate (10%) based on your audit findings, and Payment Terms & Financial Risk (10%). Calculate weighted totals and rank.

A purchase recommendation memo addressed to the Procurement Manager. Include a budget analysis against the $310,000 cap with installation and freight, a compliance summary table showing pass/fail per vendor for each mandatory requirement CR-001 through CR-010, a risk assessment covering vendor-specific concerns, a negotiation counter-offer for the recommended vendor specifying 2–3 line items or terms with target prices and fallback positions, and the applicable escalation path if the cap cannot be met post-negotiation.

All calculations must be cross-referenced between deliverables and traceable to source data.
