You are a Customer Service Representative at Lake Erie Electric Cooperative (LEEC), a regional electric utility in Erie, Pennsylvania. A January 2025 polar vortex caused electricity bills to spike, and your supervisor assigned you six open billing disputes (in the "Dispute Records" sheet of Customer_Billing_Disputes_Jan2025.xlsx) with preliminary notes (Supervisor_Preliminary_Notes.csv). Some of her observations may be incomplete or factually unsupported — you must investigate each case independently and produce three deliverables.

DISPUTE INVESTIGATION WORKBOOK (Excel .xlsx) — "Dispute_Investigation_Workbook.xlsx" containing four sheets. The "Cross-Source Validation" sheet should have one row per dispute reconciling data across the customer's stated reason, account profile, billing trajectory, and supervisor notes. The "Weather-Usage Correlation" sheet should calculate each customer's January 2025 kWh per heating degree day (HDD) ratio using summed January HDD from the weather data and compare it to their Oct–Dec 2024 baseline ratio. Benchmark each customer's January rate and bill amount against Pennsylvania statewide residential averages from the EIA's publicly available 2024 annual data, specifically Form 861 Table 5a (average monthly bill by state) and the average retail price of electricity by state. The "Regulatory Compliance Check" sheet should identify applicable PA PUC provisions from the governing PA PUC rules for each dispute and calculate business days remaining until each resolution deadline. The "Resolution Matrix" sheet should assign each dispute one of: "Valid - Adjust," "Valid - Payment Plan," "Explainable - No Adjustment," or "Requires Further Investigation," with 2–3 sentence justifications referencing findings from the preceding sheets.

CUSTOMER RESPONSE LETTERS (Word .docx) — "Customer_Response_Letters.docx" with individualized letters for all six customers. Each letter should reference account and dispute IDs, explain the bill increase with specific quantified weather data, state the resolution and next steps, and address any rate-plan-specific concerns relevant to the customer's account. Follow PA PUC guidelines for required disclosures in dispute response correspondence.

SUPERVISOR BRIEFING REPORT (PDF) — "January2025_Dispute_Briefing.pdf" presenting a prioritized action matrix ranking disputes by urgency across multiple dimensions, a summary of where your findings diverged from the supervisor's preliminary notes, any systemic issues identified across the six disputes, and two to three policy recommendations for future extreme weather events grounded in the patterns observed.
