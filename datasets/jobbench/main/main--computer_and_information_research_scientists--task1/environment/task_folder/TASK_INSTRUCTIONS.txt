You are the technical review lead at a university research computing center. The lab director submitted a GPU cluster proposal (cluster_proposal.md) two weeks ago requesting $12.5M for a multi-year HPC buildout with three hardware configurations. Since then, the provost's office issued a revised budget memo (provost_budget_memo.md) and updated vendor quotes (vendor_quote_update.csv) have arrived — both introduce constraints that conflict with the original proposal's assumptions. The original cost baseline is in budget_constraints.csv, and workload and scheduling data for the research groups is in workload_requirements.db. An ML hardware specifications dataset (ml_hardware.csv) is also available. Your job is to reconcile these inputs, assess feasibility, and produce a revised recommendation.

Produce four deliverables. First, a revised feasibility report (revised_feasibility_report.docx) that recalculates costs for the three original options under updated pricing, assesses each against current constraints, and proposes an "Option D" — a new configuration that satisfies all current requirements while maximizing research throughput, with year-by-year cash flows. Second, a resource allocation plan (resource_allocation.xlsx) with four sheets: "Project Schedule" covering 36 months of GPU allocation across all research projects and any mandated reservations, "Migration Plan" analyzing when existing infrastructure becomes unsustainable and how workloads transition, "Utilization Forecast" with monthly projections accounting for seasonal capacity variations, and "Cost Comparison" presenting recalculated TCO for all options with a cost-per-PFLOPS-hour metric using verified performance figures. Third, a SQL script (workload_impact.sql) against the workload requirements database that identifies projects at risk from procurement delays, quantifies the GPU-hours shortfall relative to current infrastructure by priority tier, and ranks projects by urgency using the formula: priority_weight × (1/deadline_flexibility_days) × monthly_GPU_hours_demand. Fourth, an executive summary memo (executive_memo.md) recommending Option D with justification addressing sustainability commitments, access mandates, a risk-adjusted procurement timeline, and benchmarking against comparable publicly-funded academic GPU deployments. For this benchmarking comparison, use the publicly available Epoch AI GPU Clusters dataset from Epoch AI; it is not included in the local task files.
