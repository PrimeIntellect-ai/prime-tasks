# MEMORANDUM

**FROM:** Dr. Catherine Osei, Vice Provost for Research, PNIT
**TO:** Dr. Evelyn R. Matsuda, Director, Meridian AI Research Lab
**CC:** Research Infrastructure Committee; Mr. David Henriksen, CIO
**DATE:** November 22, 2025
**RE:** Revised Budget Parameters and Facility Constraints for MHPACC Proposal

---

Dear Dr. Matsuda,

Thank you for submitting the MHPACC proposal. The Research Infrastructure Committee has completed its preliminary review and I am writing to inform you of several updated parameters that must be incorporated before the proposal advances to the Board of Trustees for final approval.

## 1. Revised Budget Ceiling

Due to the university's recent commitment to the new Biomedical Sciences Building (approved by the Board on October 30, 2025), the total capital allocation available for MHPACC has been reduced from **$12,500,000 to $10,800,000** over the 36-month period. The revised yearly caps are:

| Year | Original Cap | Revised Cap |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Year 1 (2026) | $5,000,000 | $4,200,000 |
| Year 2 (2027) | $4,000,000 | $3,600,000 |
| Year 3 (2028) | $3,500,000 | $3,000,000 |

The contingency reserve should be maintained at no less than 3% of the revised total.

## 2. Facility Update

The Facilities Management Office has completed an updated assessment of Room B-142. Key changes:

- **Power capacity** has been confirmed at **380 kW** (previously estimated at 400 kW). The 20 kW reduction is due to a shared transformer load from the adjacent Molecular Biology wet lab, which was not accounted for in the original site survey.
- **Cooling capacity** remains at 350 kW, but the chilled water supply agreement with the campus plant now includes a **seasonal restriction**: cooling capacity is reduced to **280 kW during June--August** due to competing demands from the campus cooling system during summer months.
- **Floor space** has been increased to **340 sq ft** following the relocation of legacy storage from the east wall of B-142.

## 3. Additional Requirements

The Committee has added two requirements for the final proposal:

a. **Energy sustainability**: The proposal must demonstrate that the chosen configuration can meet the university's new carbon neutrality pledge, which requires all new infrastructure to achieve **net-zero operational emissions by Year 2** (not Year 3 as previously stated). This may require accelerating renewable energy credit purchases.

b. **Multi-tenant access**: At least **20% of GPU capacity** must be reserved for general university research use (outside the five named projects) starting from Month 13. This reserved capacity must be accessible through the standard campus HPC job queue.

Please revise the proposal accordingly. The Committee expects the updated submission by December 20, 2025.
