You are a Training and Development Specialist at Meridian Health Partners, a mid-size healthcare organization (~120 employees across Nursing, Administration, Lab/Diagnostics, and Facilities). Your director has sent you a memo outlining Q4 2025 priorities. You also have Q3 training evaluation data and a Q4 instructor availability sheet on hand — note that these two files were compiled by different staff. Using these resources, along with other external relevant evaluation instruments (the organization previously downloaded Kirkpatrick evaluation form samples and a Smartsheet onboarding feedback form for reference), produce three integrated deliverables that cross-reference multiple sources.

Deliverable 1 — Integrated Q3 Performance & Q4 Readiness Assessment (Excel workbook). Build a four-sheet workbook. The first sheet, "Q3 Analysis," should present completion rates, satisfaction means, and pre/post score gains broken out by department, module, and delivery format; flag any module-department combination falling below 80% completion or 3.5 average satisfaction. The second sheet, "Instructor Scorecard," should compute per-instructor averages for satisfaction, knowledge gain, completion rate, and sessions taught, cross-referenced against the Q4 availability file — document any naming or credential inconsistencies you reconcile between the two datasets. The third sheet, "Q4 Schedule," should lay out a full October–December training calendar that respects every room constraint, instructor availability window, blocked date, session cap, and equipment requirement in the availability data; every department must be able to access each mandatory module at least once, and any module whose self-paced delivery underperformed in Q3 must be rescheduled into classroom or blended format; color-code sessions delivered by external (paid) instructors. The fourth sheet, "Budget Projection," should estimate Q4 external instructor costs using the hourly rates in the availability file, assuming three hours per classroom or blended session and two hours per virtual session, with per-month and per-instructor subtotals, internal staff costed at zero.

Deliverable 2 — Kirkpatrick-Aligned Evaluation Redesign (Word document). Audit the existing evaluation instruments against what the Q3 data actually captures, identify which Kirkpatrick levels are measured and which are missing, and produce a redesigned instrument covering Levels 1 through 3. The instrument should incorporate Likert-scale, multiple-choice, short-answer, and self-assessment skills checklist question types, with diagnostic items informed by the specific problem patterns surfaced in Q3. Open with an "Evaluator Instructions" preamble explaining administration procedures and how each section maps to its corresponding evaluation level.

Deliverable 3 — Director Briefing Memo with Risk Matrix (PDF). Draft a concise memo that responds to each priority in the director's Q4 memo with specific evidence from your Q3 analysis. Include a likelihood-by-impact risk matrix covering the top risks to Q4 training success, a retain/replace/renegotiate recommendation for each external consultant grounded in performance data and cost projections, and close with exactly three quick wins the director can approve immediately.
