You are a civil engineer at Larimer County Public Works in Colorado. The county is evaluating rehabilitation of Bridge No. B-42-0117 (County Road 14 over Elk Creek), a three-span steel plate girder bridge built in 1978 carrying approximately 2,850 ADT. The current deck condition rating is 4 (Poor) with exposed reinforcement. The scope memo describes the proposed full deck replacement and associated work. Your supervisor questions whether full replacement is justified or whether a partial repair with overlay could meet the $2,000,000 county budget, and wants a defensible cost comparison.

Before estimating, reconcile the scope memo quantities against independent calculations — flag any discrepancies and justify which values you adopt. For the steel painting scope, build a crew-day rate from first principles using the local rate schedule, then benchmark your result against published state DOT bid averages obtained externally. Colorado's published highway construction bid data should serve as your primary pricing reference; cross-reference against at least one other state DOT average unit price database for validation.

Prepare estimates for two scenarios. Option A follows the scope memo as written — full deck replacement with all associated work. Option B assumes 40% of the deck area requires full-depth patching with polymer-modified concrete, the remaining 60% receives hydrodemolition to 2-inch depth with patching, and the entire deck receives a thin polymer overlay. All non-deck work in Option B remains identical to Option A. Derive all quantities from the scope memo dimensions and price using the provided local references where available, along with the required externally sourced DOT bid data, building up from first principles where DOT bid data is insufficient.

Deliver a cost estimate workbook in Excel format with six tabs: summaries for each option organized by work category with 8% mobilization and 15% contingency applied, a detailed side-by-side estimate with source documentation for each unit price, a rebar takeoff showing independent verification, a painting crew-day buildup, and a price comparison of key items across multiple DOT sources with selection rationale. Also deliver a recommendation memo summarizing both option costs, identifying the three largest cost drivers per option with dollar values, flagging items with pricing uncertainty exceeding 20%, evaluating each option against the county budget incorporating life-cycle cost analysis over the respective service lives (Option A approximately 40 years, Option B approximately 15 years), and making a clear recommendation. State all assumptions explicitly.
