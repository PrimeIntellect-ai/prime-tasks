You are the Director of Finance and Administration at Riverside Community Health Center, an FQHC operating five clinic sites in central Florida. The center provides primary care, pediatrics, behavioral health, dental, and OB/GYN services to approximately 18,000 patients annually, with a payer mix of roughly 55% Medicaid, 12% Medicare, 20% private insurance, and 13% self-pay/sliding fee.

The CFO has flagged potential reimbursement discrepancies and wants a combined financial analysis and compliance package for the Board and an upcoming state Medicaid audit. You have just closed FY2024 (October 2023 through September 2024). Using the visit volume data, budget-versus-actuals workbook, and HealthFirst Family Care Center's FY2022 audited financial statements provided here, along with the CMS FQHC PPS CY 2025 rate transmittal from cms.gov and the Florida AHCA FQHC Medicaid PPS rate schedule (effective October 2024) from ahca.myflorida.com, produce the following three deliverables.

"FY2024_Rate_Compliance_Audit.csv" — Reconcile actual per-visit reimbursements against authoritative benchmarks for each service line and payer combination. Calculate annual weighted-average actual reimbursement per visit, compare Medicare lines against the applicable CMS FQHC PPS base rate adjusted for the center's geography, and compare Medicaid lines against the applicable Florida FQHC Medicaid PPS rate. Flag deviations exceeding ±5% using a PASS/REVIEW/FAIL classification. Columns: Service_Line, Payer_Type, Total_Visits, Weighted_Avg_Actual_Rate, Benchmark_Rate, Deviation_Pct, Compliance_Flag.

"FY2024_Board_Financial_Report.xlsx" — An integrated financial workbook with four sheets. The "Revenue Variance" sheet should present dollar and percentage variance for each revenue category by month with annual totals, flagging months where total revenue variance exceeds -5% in red. The "Expense Variance" sheet follows the same structure, flagging months where total expense variance exceeds +5% in red. The "Peer Benchmark" sheet should extract key financial ratios from the HealthFirst audited statements and present them side-by-side with Riverside's FY2024 ratios, identifying deviations exceeding 3 percentage points. The "Service Line Profitability" sheet should cross-reference visit volume and reimbursement data with applicable benchmark rates, showing total visits, total reimbursement, average reimbursement per visit, and a cost-per-visit estimate for each service line.

"FY2024_Financial_Compliance_Memo.docx" — A Board-ready memo summarizing financial performance with specific months and dollar amounts for key variances, interpreting compliance audit findings, benchmarking Riverside against HealthFirst on computed peer ratios, projecting FY2025 Medicare revenue implications based on the upcoming PPS rate change, and providing actionable recommendations addressing Medicaid rate reconciliation procedures, cost-per-visit reduction for underperforming service lines, 340B program enrollment feasibility using HealthFirst as a benchmark, and workforce cost optimization.
