You are the Online Store Manager for Haven & Hearth Home Goods, an e-commerce business selling apparel and home décor through a Shopify storefront. The CEO has flagged a troubling pattern: several recent customer complaints trace back to the same underperforming product lines, yet the current product listings still promote them without revision. She wants a coordinated response — not siloed reports — that connects complaint resolution, catalog accuracy, and sales strategy into one action plan.

Your first deliverable is a Complaint Triage & Cross-Referenced Response Package as a Word document. Using the complaint tickets, company policies, sales records, and product catalog, draft professional responses for all open tickets. Each response should include an internal-facing Triage Note that cross-references the complained product against the current catalog to verify whether listing descriptions match the customer's reported experience, pulls margin context from the sales data, and recommends whether to correct the listing, remove the product, or escalate — with justification.

Second, produce an Integrated Performance Dashboard as an Excel workbook with three sheets: "Profit Margin Analysis" covering Sub-Category-level profitability metrics, "Traffic-to-Sales Funnel" segmenting our website session analytics by month and visitor type to surface conversion drop-offs, and "Underperformer Crosswalk" listing the 10 worst products by profit margin and checking each against the current catalog. Our online shopper behavior data — including bounce rates, page engagement, and revenue indicators — should be pulled from the external dataset website_traffic_data.csv, sourced from the UCI Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention Dataset.

Third, create a Customer Sentiment & Catalog Alignment Audit as a PDF of 2–3 pages. Analyze customer review feedback from the external dataset customer_reviews.csv, based on the Kaggle Women's E-Commerce Clothing Reviews dataset, to compute ratings and recommendation rates by department and class, identify the 3 lowest-rated product classes among classes with at least 30 reviews (sort by average rating ascending, recommendation rate ascending, review count descending, then Class Name ascending), extract recurring complaint themes from review text for those selected classes, and cross-check whether current listing descriptions address or ignore those pain points. Provide prioritized recommendations linking catalog copy changes to specific customer complaints.

Fourth, produce a Data-Driven Product Listing Optimization as "optimized_product_listings.csv" informed by findings across all previous deliverables. Rewrite product descriptions to be accurate based on complaint and review evidence — tone down or correct claims where evidence shows overpromising. Complete all empty SEO and Google Shopping fields. Add a "Listing Change Justification" column explaining each modification with traceable references to specific ticket IDs, review themes, or sales findings.

- store_policies.docx
