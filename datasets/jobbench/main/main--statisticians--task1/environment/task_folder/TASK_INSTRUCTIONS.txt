You are a Senior Statistician at the National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS). A preliminary analysis from a previous analyst claimed obesity is the strongest predictor of state-level life expectancy, but reviewers have raised concerns about potential confounding and questioned the model specification. Your director has assigned you to audit the methodology and produce a rigorous analysis suitable for peer review.

The primary research question is whether obesity is truly the primary driver of state-level life expectancy differences, or whether this relationship is confounded by socioeconomic factors. Your analysis should compare obesity's bivariate effect against models adjusting for poverty, education, and income. The adjusted model should incorporate poverty rate data available from USDA Economic Research Service estimates and unemployment statistics from the same source. Regional clustering effects should be evaluated using states nested within the four Census regions (Northeast, Midwest, South, West).

Your deliverables include a reproducible analysis notebook demonstrating all data processing decisions, a results workbook containing five sheets (Clean_Data, Descriptive_Stats, Correlation_Matrix, Model_Comparison, Sensitivity), and a 2-3 page technical memo answering the research question with appropriate causal diagrams and discussion of limitations inherent to ecological analysis.

The sensitivity analysis should assess whether conclusions about obesity's effect remain stable when high-leverage observations are removed. Standardized effect sizes should enable meaningful comparison of predictor importance across variables measured on different scales. The final memo should provide an actionable recommendation regarding whether findings support policy focus on obesity reduction at the state level.

Data Sources:
- State-level poverty rates and median household income: obtain 2023 estimates from the USDA Economic Research Service file "poverty-estimates-for-the-united-states-states-and-counties-2023.csv" (based on the U.S. Census Bureau SAIPE program)
- State-level unemployment rates: obtain 2023 annual averages from the USDA Economic Research Service file "unemployment-and-median-household-income-for-the-united-states-states-and-counties-2000-23.csv" (based on BLS Local Area Unemployment Statistics)
- State-level COVID-19 vaccination completion rates: obtain a December 2021 snapshot from the CDC "COVID-19 Vaccinations in the United States, Jurisdiction" dataset (data.cdc.gov, dataset ID unsk-b7fc)
- Census region assignments for states (Northeast, Midwest, South, West) can be obtained from the U.S. Census Bureau 2022 regions-and-divisions map ("us_regdiv.pdf")
