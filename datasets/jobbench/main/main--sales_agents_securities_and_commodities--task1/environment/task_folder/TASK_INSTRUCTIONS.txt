You are a Sales Agent at Meridian Securities Group, a mid-sized broker-dealer. Your compliance officer has flagged discrepancies in Q4 2024 records and needs an audit-ready reconciliation package before the quarterly FINRA filing deadline. The transaction database, along with available market data files, are provided. You will need to obtain FINRA's SRO Jurisdiction Fee and Setting Schedule and Fidelity's Brokerage Commissions and Fee Schedule from their published external sources for validation and benchmarking purposes.

Produce three deliverables. First, an Excel workbook (fee_audit_q4_2024.xlsx) containing fee validation and discrepancy analysis. Recalculate SEC Section 31 fees and Trading Activity Fees for all settled sell transactions, comparing against recorded values and flagging material variances. Include a commission tier audit that benchmarks each agent's internal commission rate against Fidelity's published schedule and classifies the comparison.

Second, an Excel workbook (portfolio_risk_analysis_q4_2024.xlsx) with risk-adjusted portfolio analysis. Compute net positions per ticker for each client using settled trades. Value positions using Dec 31 closing prices from the provided market data files for AAPL, MSFT, and GOOGL; for other tickers (NVDA, AMZN, V, JPM, JNJ), derive a proxy price from settled transaction data. Assess concentration risk with a 40% single-position threshold, applying a stricter 30% threshold for Conservative-profile clients. Evaluate account suitability by computing Q4 2024 daily return volatility for tickers with market data and flagging IRA or Trust accounts held by Conservative or Moderate clients with exposure to high-volatility positions exceeding 2.5%.

Third, a PDF scorecard (agent_scorecard_q4_2024.pdf) for each sales agent covering monthly settled trade volume, net commission after all adjustments with narrative context, client retention metrics, and cancelled/pending trade ratios. The compliance notes section must perform bidirectional verification between clawback adjustments and cancelled transactions, flagging any mismatches in correspondence or amounts.
