PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS
RIVERSIDE COUNTY PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT
BOX CULVERT REPLACEMENT PROJECT

PROJECT NO: RC-2024-BC-0847
LOCATION: Miller Creek Road at Station 42+50
COUNTY: Riverside County, State of Washington

===============================================================================
1. PROJECT DESCRIPTION
===============================================================================

The Riverside County Public Works Department requires the replacement of an
existing deteriorated corrugated metal pipe culvert with a new precast reinforced
concrete box culvert at Miller Creek Road crossing. The existing structure has
reached the end of its service life and no longer meets hydraulic capacity
requirements.

===============================================================================
2. BOX CULVERT DIMENSIONS
===============================================================================

Structure Type: Single-Cell Precast Reinforced Concrete Box Culvert

Interior Dimensions:
- Clear Span (Width): 10 feet (3.05 m)
- Clear Rise (Height): 8 feet (2.44 m)
- Total Length: 72 feet (21.95 m)

Wall Thickness:
- Top Slab: 10 inches (254 mm)
- Bottom Slab: 12 inches (305 mm)
- Side Walls: 10 inches (254 mm)

Fill Height:
- Minimum Fill: 2.0 feet (0.61 m)
- Maximum Fill: 4.5 feet (1.37 m)
- Design Fill Height: 3.0 feet (0.91 m)

Skew Angle: 15 degrees right ahead

===============================================================================
3. DESIGN CRITERIA
===============================================================================

Design Loading:
- AASHTO HL-93 Live Load
- Earth Load per AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications

Concrete Requirements:
- Compressive Strength (f'c): 5,000 psi at 28 days
- Exposure Class: Class II (moderately aggressive environment)
- Minimum Cover: 2 inches (clear cover to reinforcement)

Reinforcing Steel:
- Grade 60 (fy = 60,000 psi)
- Epoxy-coated for all reinforcement

Hydraulic Design:
- Design Flow (Q100): 850 cfs
- Headwater Depth: 7.2 feet maximum
- Outlet Velocity: 8.5 fps

===============================================================================
4. PRECAST SEGMENT DETAILS
===============================================================================

Segment Configuration:
- Number of Segments: 9 segments
- Segment Length: 8 feet each (standard)
- Joint Type: Bell and spigot with rubber gasket

Wing Walls:
- Type: Cast-in-place reinforced concrete
- Height: 6 feet (varies)
- Length: 12 feet each side (inlet and outlet)
- Wall Thickness: 12 inches

Headwalls:
- Type: Cast-in-place reinforced concrete
- Inlet and Outlet: 10-inch thick parapet walls

Apron Slabs:
- Inlet Apron: 8 feet x 14 feet x 8 inches thick
- Outlet Apron: 12 feet x 14 feet x 10 inches thick
- Riprap: Class III, 18-inch thick, 150 SF at outlet

===============================================================================
5. EARTHWORK REQUIREMENTS
===============================================================================

Excavation:
- Structure Excavation: Estimated 1,850 cubic yards
- Foundation Preparation: Compact to 95% relative density

Bedding Material:
- Type: Crushed Stone Base, WSDOT Spec 9-03.9(3)
- Thickness: 6 inches minimum
- Area: Full culvert footprint plus 12 inches each side

Backfill:
- Type: Gravel Borrow, WSDOT Spec 9-03.14(1)
- Compaction: 95% of maximum density per ASTM D1557
- Lift Thickness: 8 inches maximum

===============================================================================
6. SITE CONDITIONS
===============================================================================

Existing Conditions:
- Existing Culvert: 60-inch CMP, 65 feet long
- Road Width: 32 feet (2 lanes with shoulders)
- Traffic Count: ADT 2,400 vehicles

Soil Conditions:
- Soil Type: Silty sand with gravel (SM)
- Groundwater: Approximately 8 feet below existing grade
- Bearing Capacity: 3,000 psf allowable

Environmental Considerations:
- Work Window: July 1 - September 30 (fish window)
- Temporary Stream Diversion Required
- Erosion Control per WSDOT Standard Specifications

===============================================================================
7. CONSTRUCTION SEQUENCE
===============================================================================

Phase 1: Mobilization and Traffic Control
Phase 2: Temporary Stream Diversion
Phase 3: Removal of Existing Culvert
Phase 4: Excavation and Foundation Preparation
Phase 5: Precast Box Culvert Installation
Phase 6: Wing Wall and Headwall Construction
Phase 7: Backfill and Compaction
Phase 8: Road Restoration and Paving
Phase 9: Site Cleanup and Demobilization

Estimated Construction Duration: 45 working days
