You are the Branch Financial Manager of the Riverside Branch at Pacific Coast Community Bank. It is January 2025. Internal auditors flagged that several loans in the portfolio database may carry risk ratings inconsistent with the bank's risk rating framework. Investigate and produce three deliverables for the Quarterly Credit Review.

Produce a Risk Rating Reconciliation Workbook in Excel for every loan currently rated 3 (Satisfactory) or worse in riverside_branch_portfolio.db. Re-derive each loan's rating by applying the framework's quantitative thresholds to actual loan metrics. The workbook must contain three sheets: "Reconciliation Summary" with each loan's current and re-derived rating plus a justification citing the specific framework criterion; "Migration Matrix" as a cross-tabulation of current versus re-derived ratings showing both loan count and aggregate dollar exposure per cell with row and column totals; and "Material Downgrades" with a narrative write-up for any loan re-derived two or more notches worse.

Prepare a PDF credit memo to the Senior Credit Committee for the two competing CRE applications, APP-2025-001 and APP-2025-004. Score both using the CDFI Fund's risk-rating-system-for-re-sample.xls template obtained from cdfifund.gov's Portfolio Management Resource Bank. Stress-test each by recalculating DSCR under a +200 basis point rate increase combined with a 15% revenue decline, showing explicit numerical results. Benchmark projected CRE exposure against the FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile Q4 2024 QBP Time Series Loan Performance dataset and QBP Balance Sheet dataset from fdic.gov, citing specific national delinquency rates and balance sheet figures. The memo must address CRE concentration relative to policy limits and recommend which application, if either, to approve.

For the remaining six applications in pending_loan_applications.csv, produce a Loan Decision Matrix workbook in Excel. The "Decision Matrix" sheet must score each application using the appropriate rating framework for its loan type. Each approved or conditionally approved loan requires a dedicated sheet with proposed terms, covenants, collateral requirements, and conditions precedent.
