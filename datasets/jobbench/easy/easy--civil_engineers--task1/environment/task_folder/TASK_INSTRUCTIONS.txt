You are assisting the City Engineer, P.E. with a pre-award bid review for the City of Sherwood's Schamburg Street, Storm, and Sanitary Rehabilitation project. Bid opening has already occurred, and as of January 21, 2025 the City has issued its notice of intent to award North Santiam Paving Co.'s alternate bid.

Prepare a concise, decision-oriented reasonableness review using the bid tabulation as the primary pricing record and the notice of intent to award as the current award record. Assess whether North Santiam's alternate bid appears financially reasonable for award, identify any meaningful pricing outliers, arithmetic inconsistencies, or recordkeeping issues that should be resolved before contract execution, and keep the focus on cost feasibility rather than protest procedure or contract drafting.

Send back two concise pieces: a memo to the City Engineer, P.E. recommending whether the City should proceed with North Santiam's alternate bid, including the selected alternate amount, North Santiam's base amount, the engineer's estimate, the key percentage comparisons, the main pricing drivers behind your conclusion, and the issues that should be cleared up before award; and a supporting comparison table covering the pay items or bid-record issues that most affect feasibility, with the item or issue, quantity if applicable, North Santiam's value, the comparison value used, why it matters, and the recommended follow-up for each entry.

If you conclude the alternate bid is reasonable overall, say so directly, but do not assume the bid records are error-free.
