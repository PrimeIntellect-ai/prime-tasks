You are outside counsel advising a San Francisco general purpose committee from the 2024 municipal cycle. Before the first call with San Francisco Ethics Commission enforcement staff, the client wants a practical exposure assessment and a negotiation position.

During the 90 days before the November 5, 2024 election, the committee made contributions large enough to raise Form 497 issues and may also have received contributions that do the same. The treasurer now believes the committee may have operated as a state filer for part of the cycle even though most of its activity concerned San Francisco races, and at least one contribution that should have been promptly disclosed was later omitted or misstated on a regular campaign statement.

Please prepare two work products: an internal memorandum and a short outline for the first call with Commission staff. In the memorandum, analyze the most likely reporting violations and filing-jurisdiction issues, including when the committee should be treated as a San Francisco city committee, where its Form 497s and regular campaign statements should have been filed, how prompt Form 497 reporting relates to later campaign-statement accuracy, and whether filing in the wrong place goes to liability, mitigation, or both. Use the San Francisco enforcement framework, local penalty data, and the Progress San Francisco matter to assess likely outcome, recommend a realistic penalty range, and advise on settlement posture. Address any exposure tied to incoming contributions with the factual caution the record requires. Where the facts are uncertain, state the assumptions you need and explain how different assumptions change liability, penalty risk, or negotiating leverage.

The call outline should isolate the strongest mitigation themes, the main weaknesses in the client's position, and the opening settlement range you would propose. Assume the client is not disputing that the underlying contributions occurred. Focus on practical exposure and resolution strategy rather than merits litigation.
