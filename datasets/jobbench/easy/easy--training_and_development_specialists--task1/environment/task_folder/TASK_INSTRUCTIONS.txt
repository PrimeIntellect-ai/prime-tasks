You are preparing an approval package for the director of a New York county emergency management office. Two newly appointed staff members are under consideration for the Spring 2023 National Emergency Management Basic Academy at the State Preparedness Training Center in Oriskany, and the director needs a recommendation by February 17, 2023 so she can decide whether to authorize enrollment and hold travel funds.

Provide a short decision memo recommending whether both employees should be approved for the full five-course spring sequence. The memo should map the academy blocks and registration deadlines, explain how the required Independent Study prerequisites will be completed and internally verified in time for L0101 registration, walk through the DHSES LMS account setup and course-request process for brand-new students, and identify the readiness or scheduling risks that could break the sequence. Use the attached L0101 and L0146 bulletins as examples of what the office might circulate to the employees, and state whether those materials are enough for first-year county staff or whether they should be supplemented with a brief local orientation before the April session. If you recommend a county brief, specify the topics it should cover and why.

Also include a travel budget table for both employees covering every in-person academy block in Oriskany. Use ZIP 13424 in the FY2023 GSA rate file, assume each attendee arrives the evening before a block begins and returns after the final class day, separate lodging from meals and incidental expenses, state your assumptions, and explain whether the academy bulletin's New York State lodging policy should affect the county's planning estimate or only its contingency reserve.

Keep the package practical and decision-oriented.
