You are the trial biostatistician supporting an infectious diseases team that is deciding whether to advance a follow-on TB/HIV study after the TB-HAART trial in Addis Ababa. Using the attached article and study workbooks, prepare a short statistical package for that decision.

Submit two documents: a planning memo and an investigator appendix. In the memo, assess a confirmatory two-arm parallel trial comparing antiretroviral therapy initiation 1 week versus 8 weeks after the start of tuberculosis treatment, using 48-week all-cause mortality as the primary planning endpoint. Base the planning assumptions on the observed TB-HAART results and the published methods, and state the week 8 control event rate, target effect size, alpha, power, non-evaluable adjustment, and resulting per-arm and total sample sizes. Use the published study's original design assumptions as context rather than as a substitute for the new confirmatory calculation, and briefly address whether the observed data support a narrower subgroup-focused design, such as participants with baseline CD4 counts below 50 cells/uL, while still providing the overall week 1 versus week 8 calculation.

In the appendix, include one baseline characteristics table across the randomized study arms using clinically relevant enrollment variables, one labeled figure summarizing 48-week mortality by randomized arm, and one labeled figure summarizing hepatotoxicity-related interruption of first-line TB treatment by randomized arm. Use intention-to-treat denominators unless a narrower safety denominator or follow-up window is necessary and clearly labeled, show denominators explicitly, handle missing values directly, and end the appendix with a brief interpretation of what the observed mortality and safety patterns imply for feasibility and design risk in the follow-on study.
