Prepare a short manager-ready package that explains what changed from 2021 to 2022, where the workflow is carrying the most operational strain, and what should be prioritized for 2023.

Provide two items:

For each recommendation, include the operational issue, the proposed change, the supporting evidence from the provided files, and one metric to track.
