You are a labor and employment associate preparing advice for the supervising partner. It is September 27, 2024, and a Michigan hospital-system client wants to revise its severance template for union-represented service employees after the Sixth Circuit's September 19, 2024 decision in McLaren Macomb.

Prepare an internal advisory memorandum and a clause-risk table for the partner. The memo should explain the governing framework under NLRA Sections 7 and 8(a)(1), summarize the Board's decision in McLaren Macomb and the Sixth Circuit's September 19, 2024 opinion, and assess the likely NLRA exposure if the client offers bargaining-unit employees severance terms with broad confidentiality, non-disparagement, or similar speech restrictions. Address the strongest employer-side arguments that still remain and how much weight they realistically carry for a Michigan employer after the Sixth Circuit ruling.

Use the table to give practical drafting guidance for revising the template. Use the columns `Provision`, `Why it is risky under the cited authorities`, and `Safer drafting direction`, and cover at least confidentiality, non-disparagement, restrictions on speaking with coworkers or the union, and carve-outs preserving protected concerted activity and participation in agency proceedings.

Treat this as practical management-side counseling rather than a brief. Focus on how to reduce unfair-labor-practice risk while preserving legitimate confidentiality and reputation interests, and assume the affected employees are rank-and-file, union-represented service employees rather than supervisors.
