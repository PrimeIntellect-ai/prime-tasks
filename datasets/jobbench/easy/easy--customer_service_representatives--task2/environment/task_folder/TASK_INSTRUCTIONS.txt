A customer called on February 18, 2025 about the bill issued on February 3, 2025 for January 2025 service. The customer believes the bill is too high, does not think January was cold enough to explain the increase, and asked for more time to pay while the review is underway.

Account notes confirm that December 2024 gas usage was 30 therms and January 2025 gas usage was 48 therms. Both bills were standard winter-period statements billed entirely at Tier 1, with no cross-month rate proration.

Prepare two concise deliverables: an internal case note and a customer email. Keep both deliverables professional and grounded only in the account notes and the provided references; do not assume credits, waived charges, meter problems, or other unsupported account facts.
